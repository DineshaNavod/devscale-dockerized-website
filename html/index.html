NOVA — SaaS landing page

A fictional analytics product used to explore how a technical offer can be explained clearly and calmly.

  • Hierarchy: one promise in the headline, one product visual, one primary action.
  • Proof without invention: the layout reserves space for real customer evidence once it exists.
  • Build notes: lightweight CSS charts, keyboard-friendly navigation and a mobile-first layout.

Form & Field — e-commerce storefront

A fictional lifestyle brand used to explore a product-first store with generous space and a short purchase path.

  • Product focus: large imagery, minimal chrome, clear pricing and options.
  • Purchase journey: a few clear steps from browsing to checkout, with no distractions.
  • Build notes: accessible product cards, readable type and a layout that holds up on small screens.

A fictional advisory firm used to explore how a serious service business can feel credible and approachable online.

Who we are

Small details. Thoughtful engineering. Better digital experiences.

DevScaleSolution focuses on building digital experiences that combine thoughtful design, functional development, performance and usability. We keep the process clear and the code clean, so your website works well today and is easy to improve tomorrow.

  • Design with intention.

    Every layout, colour and line of copy has a job. If it doesn't help someone understand or act, it goes.

  • Build with a solid foundation.

    Semantic markup, sensible structure and maintainable code make a site faster, more accessible and easier to change.

  • Improve through iteration.

    Launch is a milestone, not the end. We look for the next improvement based on how the site is actually used.
